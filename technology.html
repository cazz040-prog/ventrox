What Sets Our Solutions Apart

  • Broad-Spectrum Efficacy – Kills bacteria, viruses, fungi, and protozoa (including Giardia and Cryptosporidium, which are resistant to chlorine).
  • Biofilm Penetration – Breaks down biofilms in water systems, pipelines, and medical equipment where bacteria typically hide, making it highly effective in hospitals, food processing plants, and cooling towers.
  • Fewer Harmful By-products – Unlike chlorine, it does not form large amounts of harmful chlorinated by-products (like trihalomethanes or chloroform).
  • Dry, non-invasive process – Disinfects assets in place without disassembly, protecting electrical components and reducing production downtime.
